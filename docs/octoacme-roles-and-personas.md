# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Delivery Lead

### Role Summary
The Delivery Lead keeps cross-functional work moving from planning through execution. They coordinate delivery priorities, dependencies, and team alignment so the project remains on track and commitments are realistic.

### Responsibilities
- Align milestones, dependencies, and delivery checkpoints across teams
- Monitor work in progress and forecast risks to schedule or scope
- Partner with project managers to adjust plans based on progress and blockers
- Facilitate escalation paths when decisions or resources are needed
- Help ensure the team is focused on the highest-value outcomes

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with the Project Manager to maintain plan accuracy and visibility
- Coordinates with Developers and Product Managers to balance speed, quality, and priority
- Supports stakeholder communication by translating execution status into clear updates

### Goals
- Keep delivery predictable and transparent
- Reduce cross-team friction and delays
- Improve confidence in commitments and execution quality

---

## Technical Coordinator

### Role Summary
The Technical Coordinator supports technical alignment across project work. They help teams stay consistent on architecture, dependencies, and implementation trade-offs while reducing unnecessary rework.

### Responsibilities
- Identify technical dependencies and integration risks across workstreams
- Support solution design decisions and technical trade-off discussions
- Coordinate with engineering leads on implementation sequencing and quality checks
- Ensure technical decisions align with project goals and constraints
- Raise issues early when architecture, platform, or delivery dependencies are blocking progress

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Developers to clarify technical direction and implementation constraints
- Works alongside Product Managers to ensure technical feasibility aligns with product priorities
- Provides planning and escalation support to the Project Manager during implementation challenges

### Goals
- Improve technical consistency and decision quality
- Reduce integration issues and avoidable rework
- Keep engineering execution aligned to the project strategy

---

## Business Sponsor / Steering Partner

### Role Summary
The Business Sponsor or Steering Partner represents the business case and strategic intent of the initiative. They help ensure the project remains aligned to organizational priorities, funding, and stakeholder expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define business outcomes, governance priorities, and strategic constraints
- Approve major scope, trade-off, or priority decisions when needed
- Support stakeholder alignment and sponsorship across functions
- Help resolve cross-organizational conflicts and decision bottlenecks
- Ensure the project continues to deliver value for the business

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with the Project Manager to align delivery progress with strategic goals
- Partners with Product Managers on outcomes, investment decisions, and prioritization
- Provides executive backing for escalations, resource decisions, and policy alignment

### Goals
- Sustain business alignment and value realization
- Enable timely decisions and sponsorship for critical issues
- Strengthen trust between delivery teams and stakeholders

---

## Project Operations Partner

### Role Summary
The Project Operations Partner supports the day-to-day functioning of the project by maintaining process health, reporting, and follow-through across teams. They help convert activity into visible progress and accountability.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain dashboards, status reports, and project tracking artifacts
- Coordinate recurring updates, action items, and follow-up communications
- Support task hygiene, documentation consistency, and meeting logistics
- Help identify operational bottlenecks before they become delivery risks
- Promote shared visibility across project stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supports the Project Manager with reporting, documentation, and coordination cadence
- Works with Product Managers and delivery teams to ensure plans and updates are current
- Provides operational visibility that helps Developers and team leads stay informed and aligned

### Goals
- Improve transparency and consistency across project operations
- Reduce administrative friction in delivery work
- Make project status easier to understand and act on

---

## Risk and Compliance Partner

### Role Summary
The Risk and Compliance Partner helps the team identify governance, policy, and escalation concerns early. They ensure that delivery decisions consider risk, compliance, and accountability throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Track and escalate project risks, dependencies, and compliance issues
- Support policy, governance, and required review processes
- Help define escalation paths for cross-team or stakeholder concerns
- Partner with the Project Manager and Sponsor on risk mitigation planning
- Ensure documentation reflects risk status and planned follow-up actions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with the Project Manager to maintain risk registers and escalation plans
- Partners with Product Managers and Technical Coordinators to assess trade-offs and downstream impacts
- Supports Business Sponsors by surfacing issues that may affect priorities, risk, or compliance outcomes

### Goals
- Reduce surprise risk and governance issues
- Improve accountability for issue resolution
- Support healthier, more resilient project execution

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These supporting personas help clarify ownership, escalation paths, and collaboration patterns beyond the core delivery roles.
